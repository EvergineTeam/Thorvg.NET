Sample assets vendored for the LowLevelDemo examples.

From https://github.com/thorvg/thorvg (v1.1.0, MIT):
  animation.lot            (test/resources/test5.lot)

From https://github.com/thorvg/thorvg.example (MIT):
  tiger.svg, cartman.svg, car.svg, thorvg-logo-clear.svg, circles1.svg   (res/svg)
  test.jpg, rawimage_200x300.raw                                          (res/image)
  PublicSans-Regular.ttf                                                  (res/font)

Public Sans is a typeface of the United States Web Design System, licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://github.com/uswds/public-sans).

CJK subsets, for the Unicode column of the Text example:
  NotoSansCJK.ttf   Noto Sans SC  (Chinese, Japanese kana and kanji, Greek, Cyrillic)
  NotoSansKR.ttf    Noto Sans KR  (hangul)

Both are Noto fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://github.com/notofonts). The originals are 17 MB and 2.4 MB; what
ships here is a static instance at weight 400 subset to the characters the
example actually draws, produced with fontTools:

  from fontTools.varLib import instancer   # NotoSansSC only, it is variable
  pyftsubset <font> --text="<the example's strings>" --layout-features='*'

Regenerating them is only necessary if the sample strings change.
